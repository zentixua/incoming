# Incoming as a building block: universal integration (proposal)

Status: draft proposal, no code. Artem picks items; each picked item becomes its own PR.

Plan of this document:
1. Verdict.
2. What players say (store comments, videos, forums).
3. Where Incoming is a closed box today (recipes, items, launch, commands, configs, permissions, events, tags,
   data maps, damage types, scripting hooks).
4. Principle: what stays ours, what belongs to other mods.
5. Proposals by theme, each with what it enables, cost, risk, mechanism and the mods it lights up.
6. Play scenarios.
7. Roadmap and the "we do not do" list.
8. Sources.
