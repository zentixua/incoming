For Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.259 or newer. Not network-compatible with 3.2.0: update the mod on the server and for every player.

## Changes

- The fireball and the mushroom cloud are drawn as a lit volume instead of flat puffs. The fireball shines white with brighter and darker patches boiling over it, turns yellow and orange as it cools, and rolls up into the cap: one body from the first moment. The cap is a dense cloud of round billows that keeps rolling while the cloud climbs; the sun lights one side of it, and the folds between the billows stay darker. It is reddish brown at first and turns white within about a minute as water condenses in it; when the fireball touches dry land, the cap stays grey with dust. By day the cloud casts its shadow on the land.
- At night the fireball lights the land for kilometres around: white at first, then yellow and orange, and dark again within about ten seconds at 15 kt. Slopes facing it are bright, slopes turned away stay dark. For its first seconds it also lights the dust pall as bright as day, then the fires take over.
- The blast wave itself is invisible. The wind right behind it lifts dust and earth (smoke over a burning town, spray over water) and rolls it forward as a churning wall, about 150 m tall 1 km from a 15 kt burst and about 50 m tall 4 km out. At about 9 km the wall fades out, where the wind is too weak to lift dust. In its first seconds the fireball lights the top of the wall orange, even by day.
- Far away, and more so in rain, the mushroom cloud, the dust wall and the pall fade into the haze. Most haze lies in the lowest kilometre or two of air, so the cap high above stays clearer than the foot of its column; a distant cap no longer looks like a pale translucent ball.
- Light in the dust pall is computed the way light scatters in real dust: thin mist glows softly instead of standing as a white wall, and deep in thick dust it turns dusky but not black.
- Over water the air behind the wave fills with white spray instead of a brown dust pall. It thins within a minute and is gone in about three. Dust blown from the shore over the water stays brown, and spray blown ashore stays white.
- A burst on the water or just above it throws up a hollow column of water, about a kilometre high within a few seconds at 15 kt. Its top opens into a crown of spray, and right behind the wave a thin white condensation cloud hangs over the water for a second or two. Over about twenty seconds the column narrows and falls back, and the falling water rolls out as a white base surge: a bright mist in which you see only some 50 m, thinning over a quarter of an hour.
- When the blast wave reaches you, it throws your view away from the burst in one shove, and the wind behind it keeps rocking you. It used to shake the camera in a fast flicker. The tremor of the ground before the wave is weaker than the hit itself.
- After an ICBM strike, Distant Horizons terrain of the hardest-hit area turns into ruins as the wave reaches it, not tens of seconds later: the server prepares it while the missile flies. At 15 kt the far edge of that area may still show later.
- Mobs fleeing a nuclear blast run straight away from it. Before, many of them stood still for seconds.
- The client setting Mushroom cloud detail is gone. Nuclear dust pall is now called Mushroom cloud, dust wall and pall and sets the detail of all of them. OFF leaves no dust wall, pall, base surge or firestorm, and draws the fireball, the mushroom cloud and the water column at LOW.
- Flashback replays recorded with 3.2.0 play with this version, nuclear strikes included.

## Installation

Replace `incoming-3.2.0.jar` in the `mods` folder with `incoming-3.3.0.jar`. The server and every player need the same file; see the [README](https://github.com/zentixua/incoming#installation).
