# Advancements and statistics

English · [Русский](ru/progress.md)

Incoming adds its own tab, **Incoming**, to the advancement screen (`L`) and its own lines to Statistics → General.
The tab appears once you hold any Incoming item or launch your first round.

## Advancements

| Advancement | How to get it |
|---|---|
| First Strike | Launch a round of any weapon: from the designator, with a command, or from your own fixed launcher. |
| Eyes on Target | A round you steered through its camera goes off: you retargeted it with LMB in the projectile camera, or a Shahed-136 or Lancet followed a target you held in its camera frame. |
| Clear Skies | Shoot down a hostile round with a SAM you placed, or with your own weapon. |
| Still Standing | Live through a nuclear blast wave that hits you at 5 psi or more: close enough to flatten houses. You must still be alive once the wave has passed you. A sealed bunker counts; creative and spectator mode do not. |
| Lights Back On | Be in a blacked-out district when its outage ends and its lights come back. |

## Statistics

| Statistic | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Rounds Launched | every round you launch; a salvo of 6 is 6, one ICBM is 1 |
| Shahed-136 Launched, Lancets Launched, Grad Rockets Launched, Cruise Missiles Launched, B-2 Sorties, ICBMs Launched | the same for one weapon |
| Rounds Shot Down | hostile rounds you shot down |
| Nuclear Detonations Witnessed | nuclear detonations you were within reach of: where they break glass or burn skin |

## Whose credit it is

- A round belongs to whoever gave the order: you for your designator, your commands and your fixed launchers; a
  launcher fires under its owner's name, even when redstone or another player's signal fires it.
- You get credit only while you are online. A fixed launcher or salvo that fires while you are away counts nothing,
  then or later.
- Strikes from the server console, command blocks and launchers without an owner give nobody credit. Shooting down
  such a strike does.
- Your own and your teammates' rounds do not count as shot down: your SAM never fires at them either.
- Statistics and advancements are saved with your player, like the vanilla ones, in single-player, a world opened to
  LAN and on a server.
