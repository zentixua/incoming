Minecraft-derived blackout models and block states

The assets/incoming/models/block/unlit_*.json models and the
assets/incoming/blockstates/unlit_*.json block states are generated by
tools/gen_grid_assets.py from the corresponding Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1
client resources. The generator adds tint indices to model faces, sets the
cutout render type where needed, and redirects lit variants to unlit models.
Textures are referenced from the installed game and are not copied here.
