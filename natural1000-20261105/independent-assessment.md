# Independent source/raw assessment

A fresh Astra/MAX session independently inspected the exact56f production source, a0 diagnostic diff and original raw observations before reading the implementation owner's conclusions. This is a summary of its completed causal assessment, not a review of the later corrective implementation or a readiness approval. The full technical report is privately preserved, SHA256 `daf1df7ee2e2481f5d59e0b889e45d934d0a181000f1c62e0c45262bbc38a60a`.

The all-application counter, actual scar commits and aggregate stale counter establish a conservative lower bound of **7,101 non-stale admission/writability refusals** by the last clean sample. Aggregate stale includes lookahead rejections, so subtracting all of it understates this lower bound. The early interval has no ledger write or cold decoder; its refusal witness is capacity admission. Counts are attempts, not distinct chunks or an attribution of all delay to one owner. Exact independent rows and source references are in [derived observations](independent-derived.json).

The final two watched jobs have recent SEEN Tasks, one running and one queued; their age is not the age of those particular tasks. The snapshot does not include the exact failing-current operand. Immediate unchanged-state correctness, a finite mutation followed by stable retries, and the real retained flash/applied state must remain distinguishable. Raw differences between archive and stopped world show state evolution, not the cause of a particular live rejection.

FAR planning is distinct from real world application. The frozen source includes additional assembly, source-window and future-worker admission gates. At the last clean sample the future-policy estimate exceeds the resident estimate by186,548,224 bytes (nine future workspaces plus64MiB); neither is total JVM heap. The resident sample is also above the480MiB source-read threshold. Removing one forecast term therefore does not by itself prove that the next complete transition fits.

The material ledger is authoritative state, not expendable cache. Its count balance holds at the separate snapshot, but this does not certify every payload, cold import or sustained progress. The real save-phase log contains a13.021366-second frozen material interval during clean measurement; the15.140414-second explicit save interval is outside it. A frozen ledger is not necessarily a server-tick stall of that length.

The recommended bounded path is a complete funded near-consumer transition through read, validation, removal and material publication, with real cold-import and active-material/FAR controls. Focused authoritative paging becomes relevant if measured irreducible state or sustained rate cannot meet the fixed-cap obligation; the data do not establish that a broad rewrite is already required. Final actual full-cohort, movement, save/rejoin and native geometry gates remain mandatory. No physics/material relaxation, cap increase, timeout extension or reduced cohort is supported.
