# Review the scene, not just the file

Read the requested shot list below and inspect the delivered images and complete clips at their page display size.
Read the replay events, camera poses, frame-to-tick mapping and content-check report yourself. Do not use the
author's verdict or PR description as evidence. A decodable file, a source hash or a green readiness check does
not demonstrate that the shot shows its subject.

Visually inspect every image and animation referenced by the pages, including retained assets. Ask whether this
is what that picture is supposed to show and whether anything looks wrong to a player. The list below guides
that judgment; it is not a substitute for looking, and passing numeric thresholds cannot overrule visible faults.

For each asset, report the filename, frame/tick and what the viewer can actually see. Fail the shot when evidence
is missing. Inspect the beginning, middle and end of every clip, including every required phase and any edit.

Required shots:

- A normal missile launch from an original airport or another logical location on ordinary map terrain.
  No artificial platform over water.
- A factory strike with the camera on the launch side: missiles pass from behind the camera toward the factory.
  At least one missile must be readily identifiable for a continuous interval before impact at the final page
  resolution. Check its body, direction and size, not just its smoke or the explosion. Check that trimming did
  not remove the approach, and that the factory's damage agrees with the surrounding ground.
- One continuous night recording of a 5 kt nuclear strike, observed from 1.6–1.7 km horizontally. The native
  export must contain the detonation flash, fireball, visibly rising cap, developed mushroom and wave arrival,
  without skipped phases. Record several minutes; retain the full export as evidence even if the page uses
  shorter explicitly labelled excerpts. The camera must keep the relevant cloud and ground in view as it rises.
- Nuclear stills must show the phase their caption names. Verify the exact source tick and event age. A glowing
  ring at +16 seconds is not a detonation-flash still. A separate later photograph does not prove that the clip
  contains the full sequence. Check foreground objects against the pre-strike view, not just the effect.
- The targeting map must agree with current world data. No unexplained destruction seams or stale tiles.
  Do not accept cropping or replacing the site as a fix for a confirmed mod defect; identify the separate report.
- Stills from the exported clip at their actual phase, or ordinary F2; native Flashback editor keyframes and
  Export UI for motion. No film tool, generated cameras, scripted shot sequences/timers, screenshot-sequence
  animation, retouched explosions, synthesized frames, private capture/render helper or modified mod physics.
  The player's usual pack/settings in a separate copy, Complementary Unbound, 1920×1080 originals; guide UI
  visible when needed. Check the actual flash layer: F1
  suppresses Incoming's GUI-based flash as well as the HUD. Native export GUI controls must preserve the effect.
- Accurate English and Russian captions and working references in README, guide and store drafts. A caption
  cannot repair missing visual content. No store publication or release before Artem approves the new frames.

After the fresh review, place key JPEG frames in the requested local review directory and report them to Artem.
Wait for his relayed approval before merging; a passing automated or independent review does not replace it.

Report serious content failures before optional polish. Include representative frame paths in the verdict.
If a second review is needed after fixes, recheck every affected acceptance condition, including conditions
that a trim, camera change or replacement image could invalidate. Do not restrict it to file integrity.
