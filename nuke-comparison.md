# Nuclear wall: matched observer ranges

See [method, limits and proposal](OL-nuke-proposal.md). Incoming: 15 kt, ground burst, scale 1, wave 0.25. OL: FULL exact-shader preview on synthetic terrain, no equivalent yield. Day, eye 2 m, horizontal view, 70° vertical FOV, 1280×692. The terrain/lighting engines differ. Click any original image to inspect it.

Time is measured after the front reaches the point 10 m ahead of the eye. OL uses the actual Incoming frame age, not the requested filename time.
