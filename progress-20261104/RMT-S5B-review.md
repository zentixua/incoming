## F1B — P1: refreshing the near queue can strand its live assembly owner behind a new head

Relevant locations at the checked pin:
- `NuclearPrep.java:978–985`: refresh merges remaining todos with newly ready chunks and sorts all of them by distance, without preserving or releasing the current assembly claim.
- `NuclearPrep.java:1369–1375`: submission retries the new head and immediately returns on its missing window.
- `RuinContext.java:377–384`: the existing NEAR claim refuses a different NEAR centre, including one in the same context; draining callbacks does not release that claim.

Concrete supported path:
1. An explicit, unbound preparation has loaded centre C at the todo head. `requestWindow(C, NEAR)` owns the server-wide assembly, but C has no Task yet because a neighbour read or memory admission is pending.
2. Before C is admitted, a previously unready nearer chunk H becomes FULL. The normal 40-tick `refresh` (`work:813–815`; also called by `bind`) sorts H before C and resets `nextTodo=0`.
3. H is loaded, has no plan/task, and worker admission is available. Its `requestWindow` drains any old replies but cannot claim the token held by C, so it returns 1. `submit` decrements `nextTodo` and returns. Every subsequent turn repeats H; it never visits C to admit it or release its claim.
4. Choose the FAR cursor to be a different centre (or C outside the heavy-zone order). FAR cannot preempt the NEAR claim. There is no admitted C task for harvest/expiry to finish. Both centres can remain FULL, callbacks can finish, memory and workers can be free, and the finite queue still cannot advance. The new unload/unready/failure/heap releases are never reached for this valid state. A heartbeat can keep the handle alive; absent higher-priority SEEN preemption, unload, cancellation or lease expiry, this blocks this preparation and other NEAR/FAR assembly server-wide.

This is a source-established progress cycle, not a reproduced GameTest or an attribution of the earlier actual-1-Mt failure. It is in the assembly lifetime affected by the serious fix, rather than a physical solver problem. Preserve the active unadmitted assembly's place during refresh, explicitly transfer/yield it when replacing the head, or service its current owner independently of the mutable todo head. Keep late I/O/task ownership intact.

Required discriminating regression: registered explicit unbound preparation, C held before solver admission, then a newly FULL nearer H introduced through real `refresh`; complete C's old callbacks, keep C and H loaded and the handle alive, keep FAR on another centre, and require both required centres to produce actual nonempty plans without unload/cancel/external refund. The new `nuke_near_assembly_*` fixture sets `nextRefresh=Long.MAX_VALUE` (`RuinNearLifecycleGameTests.java:58`), so its passing result cannot catch this cycle.
